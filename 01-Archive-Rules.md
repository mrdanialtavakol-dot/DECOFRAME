# DECOFRAME — Archive Rules

01. Archive before Delete.
02. Preserve Original Context.
03. Keep Metadata.
04. Assign Archive ID.
05. Maintain Backup.
06. Maintain Recovery Path.
07. Keep Active and Archive Separate.
08. Review Important Archives.
09. Never overwrite historical versions.
10. Document Restorations.
11. Remove only verified duplicates.
12. Protect sensitive information.
